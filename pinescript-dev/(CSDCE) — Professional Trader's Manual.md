*   **Trader Action:** **HIGH CONVICTION SHORT.** Fade the rally. Enter short. Place stop just above the recent high. This is the classic "blow-off top" signature.

*   **Dashboard:** `MOD BULL +45.2 | ⬆ REV: CE LEAD (-1) | 🔻 UNWIND -28.4`

*   **Trader Action:** **LONG MOMENTUM PLAY.** The bears are being squeezed. As they cover their PEs, it creates upward velocity. Enter long, riding the short-covering squeeze.

## CATEGORY 3: THE NOISE / NO EDGE ZONE (Conviction < 30)
*Institutions are either not participating, or they are perfectly hedged.*

### Scenario 9: The Event Straddle (Non-Directional)
*   **Dashboard:** `NO EDGE +5.2 | ⏸ WAIT (0) | ⏸ HOLDING +1.3`
*   **Under the Hood:** Huge volume is hitting every strike, but the SAR (Straddle Activity Ratio) detected that `ΔCE ≈ ΔPE` at almost all strikes. Both sides are being bought equally.
*   **Trader Action:** **STAY OUT.** The market is pricing in volatility, not direction. Any directional move right now is a trap. Wait for the event to pass and the SAR filter to clear.

### Scenario 10: The Retail Chop (Low Participation)
*   **Dashboard:** `NO EDGE +12.4 | ✅ ALIGNED (1) | ⏸ HOLDING +4.5`
*   **Under the Hood:** Index is drifting slightly upward, but velocity across the 19 strikes is microscopic. The Gaussian weights can't find any meaningful flow. C_sign and C_flow are near zero. 
*   **Trader Action:** **STAY OUT.** This is low-volume retail noise. There is no institutional money behind this move. It will likely reverse on a dime.

### Scenario 11: The Fake Breakout (Liquidity Vacuum)
*   **Dashboard:** `NO EDGE +18.9 | ⬆ REV: CE LEAD (-1) | ⏸ HOLDING -5.1`
*   **Under the Hood:** Index suddenly spikes +100 points. Retail FOMOs into Calls. BUT, at the ATM and inner strikes, institutions are *selling* those Calls (CE velocity is negative or flat at the most important strikes). The spike has zero institutional backing.
*   **Trader Action:** **FADE THE BREAKOUT.** This is a classic liquidity vacuum or stop-run. Enter short against the spike. The index will snap back down as soon as the retail buying dries up.

### Scenario 12: The Confused Market (Cross-Currents)
*   **Dashboard:** `MOD BULL +42.1 | ⬇ REV: PE LEAD (-1) | ⏸ HOLDING +8.5`
*   **Under the Hood:** Conviction says moderate bull, Divergence says bearish reversal, Acceleration says flat. This happens when different institutions are fighting each other—some buying CE, some buying PE. 
*   **Trader Action:** **STAY OUT.** The signals are conflicting. The engine is telling you the market is fractured. Wait for Conviction to push above 60 or drop below 30 for clarity.
